HR Analytics Dashboard

This project is an HR Dashboard designed to analyze and visualize workforce data effectively.
It includes the following features:

📊 Total Employees & Attrition Rate – Track current workforce size and turnover trends.

📈 Yearly Hiring Trends – Visualize employee hiring patterns over multiple years.

💰 Average Salary Analysis – Monitor salary distribution and YoY growth.

🏢 Employee by Department – Breakdown of workforce across IT, Sales, Finance, HR, and Marketing.

🎓 Employee by Education – Distribution of employees by Bachelors, Masters, PhD, and High School.

⚖️ Employee by Gender – Gender ratio insights.

🔝 Top 5 Employees by Salary – Quick view of highest-paid employees.

🔄 Attrition by Department – Department-wise attrition rate analysis.

👉 This dashboard was built for HR analytics and decision support, enabling data-driven workforce planning.
